# Batch 04 — Services + Service detail

## Included in this batch
- `/services`
- `/services/hair-styling`
- `/services/nail-styling`
- shared service preview data and enquiry composition
- product preview visibility adjustment so the Products pages can be reviewed now

## Notes
- Service families are currently preview records that mirror the supplied page designs and copy direction.
- No durations, luxury-facility claims, hidden inclusions or appointment confirmations were invented.
- The service-detail action is strictly an enquiry, not a booking.
- Preferred date uses Africa/Lagos date logic and blocks past dates.
- The optional idea field is limited to 500 characters.
- WhatsApp messages include the service name, optional date, optional idea and the canonical page URL.

## Review guidance
- Products now intentionally show sample preview items by default, so the Product and Product Detail pages are no longer empty during review.
- Published Firestore products automatically replace the starter product previews.
- Published Firestore services automatically replace the starter service previews.
